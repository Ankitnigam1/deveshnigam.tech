Five years. One thread. Every chapter below happened in this order — small wins first, then the kind of results reviewers remember. Follow the rope: nothing here was overnight, and nothing here is exaggerated.

2021

Where it all started — the first website

It began with one question: can the internet actually pay? I built my first entertainment news website from scratch, learned how content ranks and how AdSense rewards it, and got it approved and earning. It ran for a year and a half, earning US$7,686.43 in AdSense revenue along the way — and then I sold it, for ₹2 lakh. Put together, that one website made me around ₹9–10 lakh in total. That first win changed everything: “trying things online” became a career plan.

The second website — proof it wasn't luck

A result you can repeat is a skill — so I repeated it: a second website in the same entertainment news niche. A hard lesson, learned early: never build everything on one platform.
